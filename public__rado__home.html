FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How long do I have to receive the code?

Each number stays active for 20 minutes. If no code arrives in that time, the number expires and your money is returned to your wallet.

Can I cancel a number?

Yes. Press Cancel & refund on your number and confirm. Some numbers can only be cancelled after the first 2 minutes.

How do I add money?

Open your wallet and choose a payment method: card, bank transfer or crypto. Your balance updates as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Where do I find what I bought?

Everything you buy, including account details and codes, is saved in your Orders page, so you can open it again any time.
